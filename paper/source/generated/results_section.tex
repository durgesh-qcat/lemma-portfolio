The exact and partial-credit columns tell different parts of the story.  GPT
SOL had the largest exact score, 25/60, and covered 299 of the 480 displayed
targets; its mean oracle-normalized coverage was 79.6\%.  Qwen 3.8 Max had the
next largest exact score, 12/60.  The remaining exact scores were 7/60 for
DeepSeek Expert, 6/60 for Qwen 3.7 Plus, and 1/60 for DeepSeek Instant.  Thus a
system could often identify useful candidates without assembling a maximizing
three-candidate portfolio.  Character TF--IDF likewise covered 47.1\% of
targets but found no exact optimum, which indicates that the task is not solved
by this surface-similarity rule.
