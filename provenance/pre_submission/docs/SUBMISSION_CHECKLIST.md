# MATH-AI 2026 submission checklist

Checked on 2 September 2026 against the official call for papers:
<https://mathai-2026.github.io/cfp/>.

## Already satisfied by the supplied files

- The review body uses the official NeurIPS 2026 workshop style with
  `\usepackage[dblblindworkshop]{neurips_2026}`.
- The main content occupies four pages; references and supplementary material
  follow separately.
- The review PDF and Overleaf bundle are anonymous.
- The named preprint is kept separate from the anonymous upload.
- Scores, labels, response alignment, and paper values are reproducible from
  the accompanying repository.

## Author actions before submission

- Upload the anonymous review PDF to the MATH-AI OpenReview venue by
  **6 September 2026, Anywhere on Earth**.
- Make sure every author has a complete OpenReview profile.  New profiles can
  take time to activate.
- Designate at least one author as the reciprocal reviewer for this submission.
- Confirm author order, spelling, affiliation, acknowledgements, funding, and
  conflicts before producing a camera-ready version.
- Keep the named GitHub repository private and omit its URL from the anonymous
  manuscript during double-blind review.
- If accepted, switch the style to
  `\usepackage[dblblindworkshop, final]{neurips_2026}` and use at most five
  content pages for the camera-ready paper.

The supplement may be unlimited in length, but the call notes that reviewers
may not read it.  The central task, method, scores, diagnosis, and limitations
therefore remain in the four-page main body.
